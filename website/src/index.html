01 Machines

All your machines in one sidebar.

Point Relay at a folder on anything you can SSH into: the Mac mini in the closet, a GPU box in the cloud, the laptop in front of you. Each project keeps its sessions, and every session is one keystroke away.

  • {{{icon.check}}}Bring your ~/.ssh/config. Hosts import with their Includes, Match blocks and jump hosts.
  • {{{icon.check}}}A built-in SSH client. Keys, certificates, your agent, passwords, ProxyJump — no ssh process involved.
  • {{{icon.check}}}⌘1 … ⌘9 switch instantly. Recent sessions stay connected in the background, scrollback and all.
  • {{{icon.check}}}Nothing to set up on the host. All it needs is tmux — and from 0.2, Relay can install that for you, no sudo.

02 Sessions

Close the lid. Nothing stops.

Every session is a tmux session on the host; Relay is just the window onto it. Lose the Wi-Fi, quit the app, board a plane — the work carries on, and when you’re back Relay reattaches with the scrollback you missed.

Go on, break it:

  • tmux, but invisible

    No status bar, no prefix key. Scrolling, selection and copy are the terminal’s own — tmux only keeps the session alive.

  • Still just tmux

    Sessions are ordinary tmux sessions. From any other terminal: ssh host, tmux attach.

  • Clean endings

    Archive a session and Relay runs your shutdown command — remove the worktree, stop the containers.

03 Sync

Start on your Mac. Finish on your phone.

Your hosts, projects and SSH keys sync through your own iCloud, end-to-end encrypted. Sessions don’t need to: they live on your machines, so every device finds them exactly as you left them.

One session, on every device

  • {{{icon.key}}}

    Keys that travel, or don’t

    A Relay key syncs through iCloud Keychain and works everywhere at once. Or keep one in a device’s Secure Enclave, where it can never leave.

  • {{{icon.lock}}}

    Your iCloud, nobody’s server

    Hosts, projects and trusted host keys are encrypted on your device and stored in your private iCloud database.

  • {{{icon.phone}}}

    A real terminal on iOS

    The same renderer as the Mac, a key bar with esc tab ctrl and arrows, and full hardware-keyboard support.

Relay for iPhone and iPad is on its way to the App Store. Until then, build it from source.

04 Feels local

Work remotely. Feel local.

The small things that usually break the moment you SSH somewhere — files, links, clipboard, scrollback — just work.

Drop a file, get a path

Drag anything onto the terminal. Relay uploads it to the host and types the remote path for you. Try it — drop a file on the terminal. It stays in your browser.

05 GitHub

Your pull request, in the title bar.

Relay finds the pull request for the branch a session is on, follows its checks as they run, and shows you what the session changed — without leaving the terminal.

  • {{{icon.check}}}Checks and mergeability, refreshed while they’re running, quiet once they’ve settled.
  • {{{icon.check}}}A diff tray with syntax highlighting: every changed file against your base branch.
  • {{{icon.check}}}No token needed to find the PR. Sign in to GitHub once for check status; the sign-in syncs to your other devices.
